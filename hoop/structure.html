02 — How It's Organized

League structure

All 30 teams are split into two conferences of 15, which are each split into three five-team divisions. Division and conference standings decide who plays whom in the postseason.

Conferences

The Eastern and Western Conferences each hold 15 teams. Teams mostly compete against their own conference for playoff positioning, which is what fuels East-vs-West rivalries.

Divisions

Each conference splits into three five-team divisions: Atlantic, Central, and Southeast in the East; Northwest, Pacific, and Southwest in the West. Division rank affects scheduling and tiebreakers.

Regular season

Every team plays 82 games from October to April. Final conference standings, 1 through 15, determine playoff seeding.

Play-in tournament

Seeds 7 through 10 in each conference play a mini-bracket for the final two playoff spots, a format introduced to keep more teams' seasons meaningful into April.

Playoffs

Eight teams per conference play best-of-seven series across four rounds. The two conference champions meet in the NBA Finals to decide the league title.

Draft & offseason

Non-playoff teams are entered into a weighted lottery each June for the right to draft incoming talent, a system designed to help struggling franchises rebuild.
