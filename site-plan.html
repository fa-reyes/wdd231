WDD 231 — Website Plan

Hoop History

A three-page site tracing the NBA's past, its present-day structure, and every team in the league today.

01 Site Name

Hoop History

The name plays on the double meaning of "hoop" — the game itself and the circular symbol of the sport — paired with "History," which points directly at the site's anchor page. It's short, easy to say out loud, and signals right away that this isn't just a stats site: it's about where the league came from as much as where it stands now.

02 Site Purpose

Hoop History gives basketball fans and curious newcomers a single place to understand the NBA on three levels: how the league began and evolved, how it's organized today (conferences, divisions, playoff format), and who's currently playing. The teams page lets visitors click any of the 30 current NBA teams to see roster details and a short profile, pulled live from a public NBA data source.

03 Scenarios

  • I'm new to basketball — how many teams are in the NBA, and how are they grouped into conferences and divisions?
  • I want to see the current roster for my favorite team before the season starts.
  • Where did the NBA actually come from, and when did it merge with the ABA?

04 Color Scheme

The palette pulls from the court itself at night under the arena lights: near-black backgrounds, basketball-leather orange for energy and action, and warm off-white for legibility. Every color on this page is drawn from the swatches below.

#15171C Court Night — page background
#FF6A1A Leather Orange — headings, links, accents
#22252C Charcoal Panel — card & section backgrounds
#F2F0E9 Baseline White — body text, borders

05 Typography

Oswald — headings & navigation

GOLDEN STATE WARRIORS

Inter — body text, captions, form labels

Founded in 1946 as the Basketball Association of America, the league merged with the NBL in 1949 to become the NBA we know today.

Oswald's condensed, bold letterforms read like a jersey number or a scoreboard, making it a natural fit for page titles and team names. Inter stays completely out of the way for longer passages of history and roster details, keeping everything readable at small sizes on mobile.
